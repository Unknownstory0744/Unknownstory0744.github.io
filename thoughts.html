Attachment

Attachment isn't always love. Sometimes, someone just becomes a habit.

Checking your phone every few minutes when their text doesn't arrive.
Constantly lost in their thoughts when they don't call.
Letting your own mood change depending on how they treat you.
And worst of all, when you feel them pulling away, lying to yourself and whispering, "Nothing happened, everything is fine."

And then one day you realize: you don't even miss them. You just miss the time you spent together.

Fake Love

Love each other, fight, get angry, be upset, and if you don't feel like it, don't even talk for a while.

Just never fake love with anyone.

Because anger only lasts for a short while, but fake love leaves an ache in someone's heart that they can never forget for the rest of their life.

One truth is that if life were truly that wonderful, we wouldn't enter this world crying.

Self-Respect

It is not necessary to hold onto everyone in your life. Sometimes, letting someone go is the greatest decision you can make to save yourself.

Love and Attachment

If you believe you truly love someone, keep these three truths in your heart. Often, what we mistake for love is simply possessive attachment.

1. Love gives freedom. Attachment lives in the fear of losing.

Ask yourself honestly: do you genuinely want their happiness, or do you just want to keep them close at any cost? If they don't message for a little while and your mind grows restless, if they talk to someone else and anger rises in your chest, or if they don't act the way you expect and you feel they have changed, pause and reflect. In real love, there is always space for the other person's choices, freedom, and respect.

2. True love makes you better.

If being in a relationship causes you to neglect your studies, your family, your responsibilities, or your future, ask yourself: am I growing in this love, or am I losing myself? A genuine connection never pulls you away from your life goals. It inspires you to become wiser, more grounded, and a better human being.

3. Love is about understanding, not possessing.

Attachment says, "They cannot leave me." But true love says, "I will respect their feelings and their decisions." Wanting someone is never wrong, but giving up your self-respect, your family, and your entire future for someone is not love. Attachment makes you terrified of losing them, while true love teaches you to value yourself just as much as you value them.

Love deeply, but love with wisdom. Wanting someone in your life is beautiful, but never lose yourself in the name of love. Real love does not shrink your world; it makes you greater.

Because not every love ends in togetherness. Some love stories exist only to teach us how to pray for someone even after we have lost them.

What is Love?

Love is when you care for someone without asking, "What will I get in return?"

Then why does love hurt so much?

Because sometimes you call attachment "love".

What is the difference?

Attachment says, "Stay with me, because I need you."

Love says, "Be happy, even if your path takes you away from me."

Then does love mean letting go?

Not always.

Sometimes love means holding on.

Sometimes it means walking beside them.

And sometimes, it means having the courage to release them.

So how do I know if I truly love someone?

Ask yourself one question: "Do I want their happiness, even when it does not benefit me?"

And if the answer is yes?

Then you are beginning to understand love.

And what is the highest form of love?

To love without possessing,
to care without controlling,
and to give without keeping score.

Loving an Overthinker

If you are in a relationship with an overthinker, know that nobody could ever love you more deeply than them.

And if you ever reply to their message late or miss their call on time, literally a hundred thoughts rush through their mind all at once: "What if they are cheating on me? What if someone else is trying to take my place?"

I know this might irritate you at times. You probably wonder why they overthink so much, thinking to yourself, "I love them so much, why can't they see it?"

But before you judge them, take a moment to understand why they feel this way. It is often because they have already experienced that pain in the past.

That is why they constantly need reassurance: out of a quiet fear that they might lose you. In their mind, they imagine every painful scenario where you might hurt them, yet despite all those fears, they still never leave your side.

Truth be told, being with an overthinker is a blessing. They will never walk away from you. All they will ever ask for in return is your honesty.

Borrowed from Time

The one you break yourself over every single day, terrified of losing: when were they ever truly yours, that you came to believe they belonged to you?

The one who was meant to stay beside you would have held on, even through the hardest circumstances.

The truth is, nothing in this world belongs to anyone forever.
Names, relationships, and identities are all merely borrowed from time.

The only thing that truly walks with you to the end is your karma.

So do not place the heavy burden of your expectations on anyone else.
