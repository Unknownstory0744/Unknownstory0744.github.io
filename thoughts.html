Attachment

Attachment isn't always love. Sometimes, someone just becomes a habit.

Checking your phone every few minutes when their text doesn't arrive.
Constantly lost in their thoughts when they don't call.
Letting your own mood change depending on how they treat you.
And worst of all, when you feel them pulling away, lying to yourself and whispering, "Nothing happened, everything is fine."

And then one day you realize: you don't even miss them. You just miss the time you spent together.
