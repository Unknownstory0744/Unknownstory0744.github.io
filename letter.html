The Letter

To the only one I want, in every universe.

Not Perfect, But Real

I know I am not perfect for you. I overthink so much, and sometimes my overthinking makes me ask you too many questions. Without ever meaning to, I end up hurting you, and later I sit with the regret all by myself.

But if there is one thing I want you to know: I am not fake. Whatever I am with you, I am real. I love you, and every single day I try to become a little better for you, because nobody in this world is born perfect. I admit I have flaws, but maybe those flaws are just what make me human.

That Boy

There is a boy...
who loves you truly.
Who misses you every day.
Who is scared to lose you...
who aches whenever you are sad.
He wants to tell you his heart,
but he just can't find the words.

He can't imagine life without you.
He smiles whenever he sees you.
He calls you his smile.
To him, you are everything.

And even today...
that boy still waits.
Still cares.
Still hopes.

That boy is... me.

Crying Like A Child

This was the first time in my life I cried like a child for someone. I will remember this time for as long as I live, because it was the first time I ever loved someone this deeply.

Even today, I still can’t understand how someone who felt so much like my own could become a stranger. I truly love you so much. I just wish you could realize how many times my heart broke for you.

A Thousand Fights
The truth is, even after fighting a thousand times, you were still the only one I wanted. No matter how mad we got, how much we argued, or how difficult things felt, it didn't change anything for me. At the end of every single fight, I still chose you. It was always you.
In Every Universe

"I love you. I love you in every universe."

All along I believed I would find you. Time brought your heart to me, and I have loved you for a thousand years.

You came into my life accidentally. We fell in love unexpectedly. And before I even knew it, you became my everything.

And now, I am completely yours. Mentally, physically, and emotionally. Now and forever.

Even if life forces you to leave, even if you have to go, and even if we don't end up together for whatever reason... from my side, you will always be remembered, every single day.

A Final Goodbye

This is probably my last message to you. You don’t have to reply, just read it with your heart once.

I never loved you any less. Maybe I just didn’t know how to love you the right way.

If my words ever caused you pain, please forgive me. I was always terrified of losing you, and that same fear is living inside me even now.

I pray you find every ounce of happiness you truly deserve. I will carry our memories with me forever. I know I will never be able to forget you, but I am setting you free now.

Be happy, and please take care of yourself. Alvida.
