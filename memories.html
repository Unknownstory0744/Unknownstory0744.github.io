My Favourite Stranger

Goodbye, my favourite stranger. We have become strangers once again, but this time with so many beautiful memories.

Thank you for coming into my life, and for making me feel things I had never felt before. You know, I miss your smile. Whenever you smiled, my heart finally found peace.

Destiny may have pulled us apart, but wherever you are, and whoever you are with, I truly want you to be happy. And if we ever cross paths again, even in another lifetime, beneath a different moon and unfamiliar stars, we will meet once more and write a whole new story.
