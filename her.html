About Her

A heart divided by a thousand miles and a thousand years of tradition.

The Distance
A map can't measure the divide between us. I was in the West, where the salt flats stretch into nowhere, and she was in the deep South. Between us lay a thousand miles, tied up in the rigid knots of family expectations and castes that didn't align. I wanted so badly to build a bridge to her, but sometimes love isn't enough to cross an ocean of tradition.
The First Melody
We joined a voice call for the very first time, just to finally hear each other speak. You always loved music, and that day, you heard me sing. Something shifted after that. The chats grew longer, the calls felt endless, and before we knew it, we were falling. I was captivated by your kindness, your simplicity, your innocence, and that impossibly cute tone of your voice.
The Screen's Glow
We built a world entirely in the glow of our screens. Our love started through long online conversations, bridging the physical gap with endless voice calls. I remember how you would fight with me, fiercely jealous if I spent time in a VC with other girls, even if it was just to listen to music. Your care was overwhelming, and I loved every bit of it.
The Numbers
I was younger, and you were a few years older. The world told us the math was wrong, but you would just look at me and say, "I don't care about age. I just want you." You loved me for my simplicity, for a character free of bad addictions, and you always held onto me harder than I could ever comprehend.
Tradition & Grace
In a generation rushing towards western trends, you were my anchor to tradition. You wore simple Indian dresses and sarees with such effortless grace. To me, you were the picture of a perfect life partner. But the traditional roots that made you so perfect were the same ones that tangled us: family, caste, and expectations.
The Silence
There was a short time when the weight of our friction almost broke us, and we fell into a deep silence for five or six days. I held onto my ego, refusing to send the first message. In those days, my routine was nothing but loneliness, boredom, and empty space. Yet, in that silence, we both realized the truth: we simply couldn't stay without each other.
Someone to Stay
She was the one who broke the silence, sending the first message despite my stubbornness. When we talked, the relief was overwhelming. Everything felt right again. We poured out our hearts and sorted it all out. She told me: "I will also expect right. Someone has to stay. No matter what. How much I push, they will bear and stay and calm me down. Every time I do. Sometimes I also want someone to stay."
The Friction
She wanted me to be her calm in the storm, but I was too foolish to see it. Instead of texting to calm her down when she was angry, I would stop typing altogether. I let myself get upset by tiny words, taking them as hurts rather than calls for help. I was pulling away when she was only asking me to stay.
Questions & Doubts
We used to fight a lot, and honestly, so much of it came from my own head. I overthink everything, and I would get caught up in doubts, questions, and scenarios that weren't even real. I questioned things when I shouldn't have, making small things feel huge. But no matter how bad the overthinking got, we always found our way back. We talked through it, let the anger go, fixed what felt broken, and chose to stay together.
Between The Silences
We fought so many times, and there were so many days we just stopped talking. We would stay completely silent, both of us holding onto our hurt and not texting. But even during those quiet days, we never stopped caring. We were still silently worrying about each other in our own heads. And every time, we would end up fixing it, putting the ego away, and going right back to normal like we had never stopped talking.
Eleven Mondays
When I got really sick and couldn't even come online, she was so worried about me. Being far away, there wasn't much she could do, so she kept a fast for eleven Mondays just praying for my health and recovery. Seeing that made me realize how pure and kind her heart really is. Meeting someone who cares for you like that is something I'll never forget.
Every Thursday, Forever
And it didn't stop there. Even today, she fasts for me every Thursday just praying for my happiness and well-being. When I asked her why she does it, she told me she's going to do this for me forever. Having someone pray for you like that every single week without asking for anything in return means more to me than words can explain.
Never Giving Up
Sometimes we hurt each other. There were days when things said hurt a lot, and it felt really heavy. But through everything, neither of us ever gave up on each other. Till this very day, despite all the hurt, the tears, and the hard days, we held on and never let go.
